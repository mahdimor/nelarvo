# Code of conduct

Nelarvo welcomes participation across technical, operational, public-sector,
research and worker-representation communities.

Be respectful, specific and open to correction. Critique ideas and evidence,
not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or expose private
information about another participant. Do not use project spaces to rank or
target individual workers.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive. Report conduct concerns
privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
